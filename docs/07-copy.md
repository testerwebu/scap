# Copy

## Product Lines

Your private pocket for screenshots.

The simplest way to stop losing screenshots on your Mac.

A tiny Mac app that keeps screenshots and screen recordings in one clean local pocket.

## Value Props

- Keep screenshots out of Desktop chaos.
- Use normal macOS screenshot shortcuts.
- Save new screenshots directly into Scap.
- Import screenshots and recordings you already have.
- Browse as tiles, list or list with previews.
- Select many files and move them to Trash quickly.
- Search by name, type or path.
- Your screenshots stay on your Mac.

## Main UI Copy

- Use for Screenshots
- Choose Folder
- Import Files
- Refresh
- Settings
- Search screenshots, recordings, names or paths...
- No folder selected
- No files imported yet.
- Your pocket is empty.
- Choose a folder or import screenshots and recordings to start your pocket.
- New screenshots and recordings made with macOS shortcuts will appear here.

## Privacy Copy

- Your screenshots stay on your Mac.
- Scap watches a local folder.
- No account required.
- No screenshots are uploaded.

## Avoid in V0.1

- Capture an area, window or screen.
- Scap needs Screen Recording permission to capture your screen.
- Capture failed.
- Capture Area.
- Capture Window.
- Capture Screen.

Those belong only to legacy/direct-capture exploration, not the current V0.1 flow.
