# Pricing

## Pricing philosophy

Scap should feel like a premium tiny Mac utility.

Do not price it like a cheap toy.

Do not start with a subscription.

Do not build pricing complexity before the app works.

## V0.1 pricing

Recommended launch model:

- one-time lifetime purchase
- $29 founding price
- one personal license
- no subscription
- no account

Primary pricing copy:

One tiny Mac app. One lifetime price. Yours forever.

## Founding pricing

Suggested launch ladder:

- First 100 users: $29 lifetime
- Next 150 users: $39 lifetime
- Launch price: $49 or $59 lifetime

Do not overcomplicate this in V0.1.

## Why not $9

$9 can make the product feel cheap.

Scap should be positioned as a polished, native, premium Mac utility that protects time and visual memory.

$29 is still impulse-friendly but feels more professional.

## Why not multiple tiers in V0.1

Avoid Home / Work / Family tiers at launch unless there is a license system.

Without license keys/device activation, multiple device tiers are mostly symbolic and create confusion.

Use one simple personal lifetime license first.

## Possible V2 pricing

Only after the product is stable:

- Personal: 1 Mac
- Home + Work: 2 Macs
- Family: 5 Macs

This requires license infrastructure.

## Refund policy note

If there is no account system and no license key, refunds are hard to enforce.

Use clear purchase copy:

Because Scap is a downloadable lifetime Mac app with no account system or cloud access to revoke, purchases are final once downloaded, except where required by law.

If something does not work on your Mac, contact support.
